Daily Aura Puzzle privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what data the Daily Aura Puzzle game for Android handles, why, and what you can do about it. Daily Aura Puzzle has no user accounts and no server of its own, and it never asks for your real name, email or phone number.

Who is responsible

Daily Aura Puzzle is published by Julio Alberto Pancorbo Montoro under the trade name Myrmidon Games (Spain). For any privacy question, write to contacto@myrmidongames.es.

What data is handled

WhatWhyWhere
Each day's game, your statistics (best score, highest tile, streak and games played) and your preferences (daily reminder) So the game remembers your progress Only on your device
Your Google Play Games player ID and player name, and your score in each daily puzzle The daily, weekly and all-time world leaderboard Google Play Games, if you sign in. Other players see your player name and score.
Advertising ID, IP address, device model, system and language, and ad interactions Showing ads, measuring them and preventing fraud Google AdMob (see below)
Your email and whatever you tell us, if you write to us Replying to you Our email

The game uses no analytics and does not track how you play, has no in-app purchases, and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

The daily reminder is a local notification that your own phone schedules for when the new puzzle comes out: it goes through no server and no data is sent. You can turn it on or off at any time on the home screen, and Android will ask your permission to show notifications the first time.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Daily Aura Puzzle is free and funded by a Google AdMob banner that only appears at the bottom of the screen while you play. There are no videos or full-screen ads. Google and its partners may use your device's advertising ID and other technical data to show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before loading ads. You can change your choice at any time in Ad choices on the home screen. If you don't consent to personalised ads, the ads you see won't be based on your interests.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Google Play Games

The leaderboard only works if you are signed in to Google Play Games; if you are not, your score never leaves your phone. Google stores that data under its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Legal basis

Saving your game and posting your score to the leaderboard are necessary to provide the service you ask for when you play and sign in. Personalised ads are based on your consent. Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention are based on our legitimate interest in keeping the game free and safe. Replying to your messages is based on your own request.

How long data is kept

Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings; the game only keeps the games of the last few days. Your leaderboard scores stay in your Google Play Games account until you delete the game's data from the Google Play Games app. Emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request. Google keeps ad data under its own policy.

Children

Daily Aura Puzzle is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you think a child has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to access, rectify, erase, restrict or port your data, and object to its processing, by writing to contacto@myrmidongames.es. As we have no server of our own, you manage much of that data directly:

  • data on your device is deleted by uninstalling the game;
  • leaderboard scores are deleted from Google Play Games (Profile → Settings → Delete game data);
  • ad consent is withdrawn in Ad choices on the home screen.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here with its date. If the change is important, we will also let you know inside the game.
