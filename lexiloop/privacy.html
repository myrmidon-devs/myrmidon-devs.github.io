LexiLoop privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what data the game LexiLoop for Android handles, why, and what you can do about it. LexiLoop does not ask for your real name, email or phone number: you play with a nickname of your choice.

Who is responsible

LexiLoop is published by Julio Alberto Pancorbo Montoro under the trade name Myrmidon Games (Spain). For any privacy question write to contacto@myrmidongames.es.

What other players see

  • Your friends see your nickname, colour, @username, level, streak, rank and scores. If you turn off Settings → Appear in rankings, you no longer show up in their rankings.
  • Anyone who knows your @username can find you to send a friend request. If you turn off Settings → Let people find me by @username, only people with your link can do so.
  • When you challenge someone with "Share link", the link contains your nickname, colour, scores and the words you found, so the other player can play the same challenge. That link does not go through our servers: you share it with whoever you want.

Ads (Google AdMob)

LexiLoop is free and funded by Google AdMob ads: optional videos in exchange for in-game help, and an occasional ad between levels. Google and its partners may use your device's advertising ID and other technical data to show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. If you do not consent, the ads you see will not be personalised.
  • You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

You can buy "Remove ads" and exclusive cosmetics with real money. Payment is always handled by Google Play: we never see your card or payment details. To know what you have bought we use RevenueCat, which receives the Google Play purchase receipt and an anonymous identifier created by the game; it does not receive your name or email. More information: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Notifications and cloud save (Google)

  • Challenge alerts: if you allow notifications, the game registers your phone with Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging and stores that identifier in Supabase so it can notify you. You can turn it off in Settings → Friend challenges (the identifier is deleted from the server) or remove the permission in Android settings.
  • Cloud save: if you connect it, your game is saved to Google Play Games with your Google account. We never see your Google account or email. You can delete that copy from the Google Play Games app.
  • Android backup: if it is turned on on your phone, Android keeps a copy of your game in your Google account to restore it when you reinstall or change phone.
  • More information: policies.google.com/privacy.

Legal basis

Storing your game, profile, friends and scores is necessary to provide the service you ask for when you play. Checking that games are possible and keeping the server secure rely on the legitimate interest of keeping rankings fair. Personalised ads rely on your consent; non-personalised ads and fraud prevention, on the legitimate interest of keeping the game free and safe. Purchases rely on the purchase contract you accept when paying. Challenge alerts and cloud save rely on your consent when you turn them on. Replying to your messages relies on your own request.

How long data is kept

Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game; copies in Google Play Games and in the Android backup stay in your Google account until you delete them. Data in Supabase (profile, friends, scores and challenges) is kept while you have the account, and is deleted as soon as you choose Settings → Delete account. Uninstalling the game does not delete the account on the server: do it first from Settings, or write to us with your @username and we will delete it. Emails are kept for as long as needed to handle your request. Google keeps ad data according to its own policy.

Children

LexiLoop is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you believe a child has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can request access to, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, and object to its processing, by writing to contacto@myrmidongames.es. You can delete your account and all its data yourself in Settings → Delete account. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here with its date. If the change is significant, we will also announce it in the game.
