Amaze Dog! privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what data the game Amaze Dog! for Android handles, why, and what you can do about it. Amaze Dog! has no user accounts and no server of its own, and never asks for your email or phone number.

Who is responsible

Amaze Dog! is published by Julio Alberto Pancorbo Montoro under the trade name Myrmidon Games (Spain). For any privacy question, write to contacto@myrmidongames.es.

What data is handled

WhatWhyWhere
Your progress (levels, stars, daily challenge, streak, missions, achievements, stats, bones, shop items and album), your profile name, avatar and frame, and your settings So the game remembers your progress Only on your device
A copy of your progress and profile (without settings) To restore it on another phone or after reinstalling, and to merge it if you play on several devices In your Google Play Games account, if you use it. Only this app can read it.
Your Google Play Games player name and picture, your star total and a tag with the avatar and frame you are wearing The global star ranking Google Play Games, if you sign in. Other players can see your name, picture and score.
Advertising ID, IP address, device model, OS and language, and ad interactions To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud Google AdMob (see below)
Your in-game purchases (product, date, price and Google Play receipt) and an anonymous purchaser ID To give you what you bought and restore it with “Restore purchases” Google Play and RevenueCat (see below)

The name you type in your profile is never sent to the ranking: your Google Play Games name is shown there instead. The game uses no analytics, does not track how you play, and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

When you share your daily challenge result, the game only prepares a text and opens your phone’s share menu: you choose whether to send it and to whom. The WhatsApp stickers are bundled in the app; when you tap “Add to WhatsApp”, WhatsApp asks you to confirm and copies the stickers, and the game only asks WhatsApp whether you already have that pack. If you rate the game, the rating is handled by Google Play.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Amaze Dog! is free and funded by Google AdMob ads: optional videos in exchange for continuing with an extra paw, a hint or extra bones, and at most one ad between levels. There are no banners and no ads during play. Google and its partners may use your device’s advertising ID and other technical data to show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before loading ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. If you do not consent to personalised ads, the ads you see will not be based on your interests.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • If you buy “Remove ads” or the “Welcome pack”, the ad between levels stops appearing; the optional videos remain available if you want to watch them.
  • More on how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

You can buy “Remove ads”, the “Welcome pack” and bags of bones with real money. Payment is always handled by Google Play: we never see your card or payment details. To know what you have bought we use RevenueCat, which receives the Google Play purchase receipt, basic device data and an anonymous ID; it does not receive your name or email. More information: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Google Play Games

The cloud copy and the ranking only work if you are signed in to Google Play Games; if you are not, they are not created and your progress stays only on your phone. Google stores that data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Legal basis

Saving your progress, the cloud copy and the ranking are necessary to provide the service you ask for when you play and sign in. Personalised ads are based on your consent. Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention are based on our legitimate interest in keeping the game free and safe. Purchases are based on the purchase contract you accept when paying. Replying to your messages is based on your own request.

How long data is kept

Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data from Android settings. The cloud copy and your ranking score stay in your Google Play Games account until you delete the game’s data from the Google Play Games app. Emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request. Google and RevenueCat keep their data according to their own policies.

Children

Amaze Dog! is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you believe a child has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, and object to its processing, by writing to contacto@myrmidongames.es. As we have no server of our own, you can manage much of this data directly:

  • data on your device is deleted by uninstalling the game;
  • the cloud copy and the ranking are deleted from Google Play Games (Profile → Settings → Delete game data);
  • consent for ads is withdrawn in Settings → Ad privacy;
  • for purchase data, write to us and we will ask RevenueCat to delete your anonymous ID.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or to the data protection authority of your country.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here with its date. If the change is significant, we will also let you know inside the game.
