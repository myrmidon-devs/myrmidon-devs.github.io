Delivery Run Race privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what data the Delivery Run Race game for Android handles, why, and what you can do about it. Delivery Run Race has no user accounts and no server of its own, and it never asks for your real name, email or phone number.

Who is responsible

Delivery Run Race is published by Julio Alberto Pancorbo Montoro under the trade name Myrmidon Games (Spain). For any privacy question, write to contacto@myrmidongames.es.

What data is handled

WhatWhyWhere
Your progress (levels, stars, coins, endless-mode record, upgrades, missions, streak, achievements and weekly event), your cosmetics, your profile name, avatar and frame, and your settings (volume, music, vibration and language) So the game remembers your progress Only on your device
Advertising ID, IP address, device model, system and language, and ad interactions Showing ads, measuring them and preventing fraud Google AdMob (see below)
Your in-game purchases (product, date, price and purchase receipt) Giving you what you buy and restoring it if you reinstall the game Google Play (see below)
Your email and whatever you tell us, if you write to us Replying to you Our email

The name you type in your profile is stored only on your phone: it is never sent anywhere. The game uses no analytics and does not track how you play, has no online leaderboards, and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Delivery Run Race is free and funded by Google AdMob ads: optional videos that you choose to watch (revive after a crash, multiply your end-of-run coins, the free box or extra coins) and, now and then, an ad when a run ends. There are never ads in the middle of a run. Google and its partners may use your device's advertising ID and other technical data to show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before loading ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. If you don't consent to personalised ads, the ads you see won't be based on your interests.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • If you buy "Remove ads", the ad between runs stops appearing; the optional videos are still there if you want to watch them.
  • More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Google Play)

You can buy "Remove ads", the "Starter pack" and coin bundles with real money. Payment is always handled by Google Play: we never see your card or payment details. The game only asks Google Play what you have bought, to give you the product and restore it if you reinstall the game. Google handles that data under its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Legal basis

Saving your progress is necessary to provide the service you ask for when you play. Personalised ads are based on your consent. Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention are based on our legitimate interest in keeping the game free and safe. Purchases are based on the purchase contract you accept when you pay. Replying to your messages is based on your own request.

How long data is kept

Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings. Emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request. Google keeps ad and purchase data under its own policy.

Children

Delivery Run Race is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you think a child has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to access, rectify, erase, restrict or port your data, and object to its processing, by writing to contacto@myrmidongames.es. As we have no server of our own, you manage much of that data directly:

  • data on your device is deleted by uninstalling the game;
  • ad consent is withdrawn in Settings → Ad privacy;
  • your purchase history is available in your Google Play account.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here with its date. If the change is important, we will also let you know inside the game.
