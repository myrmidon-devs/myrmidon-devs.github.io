Pawdoku privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what data the game Pawdoku for Android and iOS handles, why, and what you can do about it. Pawdoku has no user accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number.

Who is responsible

Pawdoku is published by Julio Pancorbo under the trade name Myrmidon Games (Spain). For any privacy question, write to contacto@myrmidongames.es.

What data is handled

WhatWhyWhere
Your progress and settings (levels, stars, treats, items, language, volume…) So the game remembers your progress Only on your device
A copy of your progress To restore it on another phone or after reinstalling In your Google Play Games account, if you use it. We do not receive your name or email.
Advertising ID, IP address, device model and system, and interactions with ads To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud Google AdMob (see below)
Your email address and what you tell us, if you write to us from "Send feedback" To answer you Our mailbox

The game's reminders are local notifications: they are created on your own device and send no data anywhere. If you rate the app from the game, the rating is handled by Google Play.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Pawdoku is free and is funded by Google AdMob ads: optional videos in exchange for a hint, a dog or getting your lives back, and an ad between levels every now and then. Google and its partners may use your device's advertising ID and other technical data to show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. Without your consent, the ads you see are not personalised.
  • You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Legal basis

Saving your progress and its cloud copy is needed to provide the service you ask for when you play. Personalised ads are based on your consent. Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention are based on the legitimate interest of keeping the game free and safe. Answering your messages is based on your own request.

How long data is kept

Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. The cloud copy stays in your Google Play Games account until you delete it from the Google Play Games app. Emails are kept as long as needed to answer you. Google keeps ad data according to its own policy.

Children

Pawdoku is suitable for everyone but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you believe a child has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and object to its processing, by writing to contacto@myrmidongames.es. You can also withdraw your consent for ads in Settings → Ad privacy. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or to the authority of your country.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with its date. If the change is significant, we will also let you know in the game.
