Regiones privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what data the Regiones game for Android handles, why, and what you can do about it. Regiones has no user accounts and no server of its own, and it never asks for your name, email or phone number.

Who is responsible

Regiones is published by Julio Alberto Pancorbo Montoro under the trade name Myrmidon Games (Spain). For any privacy question, write to contacto@myrmidongames.es.

What data is handled

WhatWhyWhere

Your Google Play Games player name and picture, your achievements, your total stars and the endless puzzles you have solved Achievements and leaderboards Google Play Games, if you sign in. Other players see your name, picture and score on the leaderboards.

Your email and whatever you tell us, if you write to us Replying to you Our email

The game uses no analytics and does not track how you play, and it does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

  • The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled by your own phone: it goes through no server. It is off until you turn it on in Settings, and Android will ask your permission to show notifications.
  • When you share a result, the game only prepares a text with the board drawn in emojis and opens your phone's share menu: you choose whether to send it and to whom.
  • After a few levels, the game may ask you for a rating. Google Play handles it: we don't see who rates or what they write until it is published on the store.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Regiones is free and funded by Google AdMob ads: a banner at the bottom while you play, an ad now and then when you finish a level, and optional videos that you choose to watch in exchange for hints. Google and its partners may use your device's advertising ID and other technical data to show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before loading ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. If you don't consent to personalised ads, the ads you see won't be based on your interests.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • If you buy "Remove ads" or "Premium", the banner and the ads between levels stop appearing.
  • More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Google Play)

You can buy "Premium", "Remove ads" and hint packs with real money. Payment is always handled by Google Play: we never see your card or payment details. The game only asks Google Play, on your own phone, what you have bought, to give you the product and restore it with "Restore purchases". Google handles that data under its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Google Play Games

Achievements and leaderboards only work if you are signed in to Google Play Games; if you are not, nothing is sent and your progress stays on your phone. Google stores that data under its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Legal basis

Saving your progress, achievements and leaderboards are necessary to provide the service you ask for when you play and sign in. Personalised ads are based on your consent. Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention are based on our legitimate interest in keeping the game free and safe. Purchases are based on the purchase contract you accept when you pay. Replying to your messages is based on your own request.

How long data is kept

Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings. If Android backup is turned on, the system may include it in your Google account backup to restore it on another phone. Achievements and scores stay in your Google Play Games account until you delete the game's data from the Google Play Games app. Emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request. Google keeps ad and purchase data under its own policy.

Children

Regiones is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you think a child has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to access, rectify, erase, restrict or port your data, and object to its processing, by writing to contacto@myrmidongames.es. As we have no server of our own, you manage much of that data directly:

  • data on your device is deleted by uninstalling the game;
  • achievements and scores are deleted from Google Play Games (Profile → Settings → Delete game data);
  • ad consent is withdrawn in Settings → Ad privacy;
  • your purchase history is available in your Google Play account.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here with its date. If the change is important, we will also let you know inside the game.
